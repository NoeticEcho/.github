# Code of conduct

NoeticEcho's projects follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short, be kind, be precise, and assume good faith. Harassment and personal
attacks are not tolerated, in any form.

**Reporting.** Please report a problem privately, through the contact form at
https://noeticecho.space. Do not use a public issue. Reports are read by the
maintainers and kept confidential. We will tell you what we did.

The Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban,
permanent ban) are how we respond.
